## 4. Lanham Act Trademark Notice for Domain Impersonation

When infringing parties register deceptive typo-squatted domains (e.g., `sendput-ai.com`, `sendput-engine.net`, `sendputaudit.com`), file an expedited UDRP complaint with ICANN or issue an immediate Lanham Act § 43(a) Notice to the registrar for immediate domain suspension on grounds of fraudulent consumer misrepresentation.
