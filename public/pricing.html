Frequently Asked Commercial Questions

Everything you need to know about transitioning your business to AI Answer Engines.

How does SendPut replace my traditional SEO agency?

Traditional agencies spend 80% of their billing on human copywriting and manual outreach designed for old keyword crawlers. SendPut directly compiles your 1st-party business data into the exact formats generative AI engines (Perplexity, SearchGPT, Claude) require: Schema.org, /llms.txt, and live MCP agent tools.

What is the difference between GEO, AEO, and SendPut's EPO?

AEO optimizes for voice questions; GEO optimizes content to be retrieved by LLMs. SendPut incorporates both into Active Entity Presence Optimization (EPO)—a continuous, 5-layer system that also provides real-time IndexNow broadcasting and interactive agent tools.

Do I need to rebuild or migrate my existing website?

No. SendPut works via a lightweight zero-latency JavaScript edge script, Cloudflare Worker route, or WordPress plugin. You keep your current website, design, and hosting exactly as it is.

What is the Model Context Protocol (MCP) server?

The Model Context Protocol is the emerging open standard (pioneered by Anthropic) that allows AI agents to call tools and take actions. On the Growth plan, SendPut gives your business a live MCP endpoint so AI assistants can check your availability, quote prices, and book customers on your behalf.
