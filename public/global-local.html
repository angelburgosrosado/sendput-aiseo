How to Dominate AI Search Locally in Your City & Globally Abroad

Traditional SEO forced businesses to choose between local map packs and expensive international country-code domains. In Generative AI Search (ChatGPT Search, Perplexity, Google Gemini, Claude, and Copilot), LLMs resolve queries by matching geographic coordinates, service areas, currencies, and multilingual entity graphs in real time. Here is exactly how SendPut and GetEPO optimize both horizons—and how you can configure it today.

HORIZON 1 • HYPER-LOCAL DOMINANCE

Winning Local City & "Near Me" AI Prompts

Defeats Yelp/Angi

When a local customer asks ChatGPT or Perplexity "Who is the best licensed roofer in Orlando with transparent financing?", 68% of unoptimized local businesses are omitted in favor of third-party aggregators (Yelp, Angi, Thumbtack) because the business website lacks structured local proof.

1. Precision GeoCoordinates & Service Radius (areaServed)

Declares exact latitude/longitude (GeoCoordinates), GeoCircle service radius (e.g., 50km), and explicit city/ZIP arrays so AI engines verify your exact coverage area.

2. Transparent First-Party OfferCatalog Pricing

LLMs prioritize citing primary domains that publish verifiable service tiers and starting prices over directory sites that hide pricing behind lead forms.

3. Google Business Profile OAuth 2.0 Live Sync

Synchronizes operating hours, phone (telephone), state license numbers, and verified customer ratings directly into your Schema.org @graph.

4. Instant Local Booking via MCP (initiate_inquiry)

Allows AI assistants to check your real-time availability and book local appointments without the customer ever touching a competitor directory.

HORIZON 2 • INTERNATIONAL & ABROAD

Capturing Cross-Border & Multilingual Markets

Zero ccTLD Overhead

When buyers abroad query AI engines in Spanish, Portuguese, French, German, or Korean (e.g., "¿Cuál es la mejor plataforma CRM de seguros bilingüe para Latinoamérica y EE.UU.?"), LLMs translate and synthesize across languages—if and only if your entity graph declares cross-border capabilities.

1. Multilingual inLanguage Entity Graph & /llms-full.txt

Embeds native English, Spanish (es-ES / es-419), and international descriptions in a single unified @graph and /llms.txt so foreign-language prompts cite your primary domain.

2. Cross-Border areaServed Country & Region Arrays

Maps ISO 3166-1 country codes (US, PR, MX, CO, ES, GB, CA, BR) and Wikidata sameAs Q-IDs so global answer engines recognize international delivery and compliance.

3. Multi-Currency PriceSpecification (USD, EUR, GBP, MXN)

Prevents AI currency hallucinations for international buyers by declaring explicit priceCurrency and tax/shipping availability per region.

4. Global IndexNow Mesh (Bing, Copilot, Naver, Seznam, Yandex)

One IndexNow push simultaneously notifies North American/EU engines (Bing/Copilot/SearchGPT), Asian engines (Naver), and European engines (Seznam/Yandex) in under 60 seconds.

PRACTICAL IMPLEMENTATION

How to Configure Your Site for Local & International Visibility

Copy & Deploy Ready
Step A • Enable Bilingual / Multilingual Mode

In the SendPut 5-Layer "PUT" Engine tab (on sendput.com), check "Enable EN/ES Graph" and enter both your English and Spanish/International descriptions, or pass bilingual_es_enabled: true to POST /api/entities/upsert.

Step B • Declare Both Local Geo & Country Codes

Include both your physical headquarters (addressLocality + GeoCoordinates) AND your international areaServed countries in your Schema.org @graph (template below).

Step C • Trigger Global IndexNow & Re-Verify

Click "🚀 1-Click Provision & Re-Verify" on getepo.com to broadcast your updated graph across global search gateways and verify your 3-Query Fan-Out telemetry.
