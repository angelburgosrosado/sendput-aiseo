STEP 01

Run a Deep 5-Layer AST Diagnostic on GetEPO.com

Endpoint: POST /api/audit

Enter your business name, canonical URL, and primary market (local city or international sector) on GetEPO.com. The Deep AST Protocol Inspector performs a real-time inspection across five layers:

1. /robots.txt

Verifies explicit Allow: / rules for all 8 AI retrieval bots (OAI-SearchBot, PerplexityBot, ClaudeBot, Google-Extended, etc.).

2. Schema.org AST

Parses <script type="application/ld+json"> and /schema.json for @graph, @id, sameAs, and OfferCatalog.

3. IndexNow Key

Checks root cryptographic key verification for sub-minute Bing, Copilot, ChatGPT Search, Naver & Yandex indexing.

4. /llms.txt Budget

Measures token density (< 2,000 tokens for /llms.txt) and checks /llms-full.txt for deep RAG ingestion.

5. MCP 2026-07-28

Detects live JSON-RPC 2.0 / SSE Model Context Protocol servers for autonomous agent tool execution.
